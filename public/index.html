Affective Echoes

Welcome, and thank you for taking part.

Over the next half hour, you'll have two conversation blocks with Alex about several everyday topics.

  1. A quiet moment first. You'll sit and rest for one minute while the equipment settles.
  2. Short conversations. Speak with Alex as you would on a video call. Replies must be spoken; typing is disabled. Please speak for at least 10 seconds in each answer so the emotion model has enough context. Alex waits until you've finished and stops to listen if you start talking.
  3. Quick check-ins. Now and then a few sliders will ask how you feel. The conversation pauses while you answer. There are no right or wrong answers.
  4. Short breaks. A note at the top of the screen lets you know before each break.

Throughout the conversations, please pay attention to any changes in your own emotions.

You can stop at any time, for any reason, with the Stop button at the top of the screen or by telling the researcher. There is no penalty for stopping.

Sit comfortably and rest your eyes on the cross.
